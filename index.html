我把个人网页看作一本可不断更新的视觉杂志：这里收藏设计作品、灵感片段、品牌观察和关于奢侈品文化的思考。我的兴趣不只停留在“好看”，而是想理解图像、材料、身份和消费心理如何一起塑造品牌故事。

English Bio

I see this website as an evolving visual magazine: a space for design projects, inspiration fragments, brand observations, and thoughts on luxury culture. I am interested in how image, material, identity, and consumer psychology shape a brand narrative.

Evaaaaa's note / 置顶观点

What makes luxury feel alive?

我想讨论一个问题：奢侈品的价值只来自价格、工艺和品牌历史吗？还是也来自它如何参与我们的身份表达、情绪记忆和文化想象？

I want to ask: does luxury value only come from price, craft, and heritage, or also from the way it shapes identity, emotion, and cultural imagination?
